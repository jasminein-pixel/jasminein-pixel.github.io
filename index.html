01 / TEAM PROJECT · DESKTOP APPLICATION2026

A desktop research-collaboration platform that helps students discover faculty projects, build profiles, upload CVs, apply for opportunities, and track applications. Faculty can publish projects and review applicants. Its recommendation engine weighs technical skills (70%), research interests (20%), and preferences (10%) to rank project fit.

My contribution: Research into existing platforms, related works, project motivation, and procedures and methods.

02 / PYTHON · VOICE-CONTROLLED DESKTOP ASSISTANTREFINED 2026

CompuBrain

A voice-controlled desktop assistant written in Python. It listens to spoken commands and responds with speech, with capabilities for web searches, opening frequently used sites, playing YouTube media, summarizing Wikipedia articles, telling jokes, sharing the time, and saving timestamped reminders locally. Originally created for a Grade 9 ICT Exhibition, it was refactored in 2026 into a modular Python package with separate skill modules, a command router, tests, and packaging.

Python 3.9+Google Speech Recognitionpyttsx3Modular designPytest

03 / FRONTEND · RESTAURANT E-COMMERCE CONCEPT2024

Binar

A restaurant e-commerce front-end concept featuring a food-focused landing page, separate menu pages, and a contact route. Visitors can navigate non-vegetarian, vegetarian, and vegan categories, browse food listings and displayed prices, and interact with order-now calls-to-action. The site uses HTML, CSS/SCSS, JavaScript, and GSAP for its front-end presentation; the repository does not establish a connected payment or ordering backend.

04 / PRODUCT CONCEPT · IN PROGRESS2026

QueueLess

A campus-canteen ordering and token-management concept aimed at reducing time spent waiting in line. The proposed flow combines digital ordering, token status, and estimated waiting times so students can plan collection more easily. Currently at the idea and planning stage.
